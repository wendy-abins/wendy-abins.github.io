Accessibility Statement

A&B Insurance Group, LLC is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to ensure our services are available to all members of our community.

Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA. A&B Insurance Group, LLC aims to be conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA.

Measures to Support Accessibility

We take the following measures to ensure accessibility:

  • Integration of semantic HTML and ARIA landmarks across all pages.
  • Implementation of "Skip to Main Content" functionality for keyboard users.
  • Ensuring all images have descriptive alternative text.
  • Regularly reviewing the site for broken links and accessibility barriers.
